Get in Touch ↗

I specialize in tailored digital solutions for businesses, founders, and creators across several core disciplines:

  • • Portfolio & Showcase Websites: Custom portfolios, editorial layouts, and interactive experiences designed to highlight creative and professional work.
  • • Business Platforms & Landing Pages: Structured commercial websites, product catalogs, and high-converting pages built for speed and clarity.
  • • Visual Brand Identities: Distinctive logomarks, typography scales, color palettes, and brand guidelines.
  • • Graphic Design & Marketing Assets: Promotional graphics, presentation collateral, and high-impact digital artwork tailored for web and social.

Every engagement follows a structured five-step process to ensure clarity, collaboration, and high-quality results:

  • • 1. Understand: We discuss your business goals, target audience, brand direction, and core project requirements.
  • • 2. Plan: We map out page structure, project milestones, and agreed delivery timelines.
  • • 3. Create: I design clean layouts and build responsive interfaces with regular progress updates.
  • • 4. Review: We test across screen sizes, incorporate your feedback, and refine every detail.
  • • 5. Deliver: Final production files, code, and assets are handed over ready for launch.

I work with a modern, production-tested technology stack designed for performance, scalability, and design fidelity:

  • • Frontend Core: Next.js, React, JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and HTML5 semantic markup.
  • • Motion & Canvas: Lenis Smooth Scroll, Three.js WebGL shaders, and CSS GPU-accelerated micro-interactions.
  • • Design & Creative Suite: Figma for design systems and wireframes; Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop for visual assets.
  • • Hosting & Infrastructure: Vercel cloud hosting with global edge network, custom DNS routing, SSL certificates, and GitHub CI/CD workflows.

Project timelines vary based on scope, feature set, and content readiness:

  • • Landing Pages & Portfolios: Typically 1 to 2 weeks from kickoff to final live deployment.
  • • Business Platforms & Catalogs: 2 to 4 weeks for multi-page architectures, product category databases, and custom interactions.
  • • Transparent Delivery: Weekly scheduled milestone check-ins, live staging links, and direct communication ensure we launch on schedule.

Getting started is simple, transparent, and hassle-free:

  • • 1. Send an Inquiry: Fill out the contact form below or reach out directly at kgill3662@gmail.com.
  • • 2. Share Project Details: Provide a brief summary of your project, target launch date, required features, and any reference designs.
  • • 3. Proposal & Timeline: I will review your requirements and provide a tailored proposal, timeline estimate, and next steps within 24 hours.
