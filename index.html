I design and engineer tailored digital experiences across five dedicated disciplines:

  • • Business & Commercial Platforms: Full-featured websites for companies and retail businesses (like Supreme Infosys) with structured product catalogs, technical hardware specifications, multi-device accessibility, and streamlined customer inquiries.
  • • Personal Portfolios & Landing Pages: High-impact, responsive web showcases featuring brutalist editorial typography, smooth scrolling motion, and interactive case study cards.
  • • UI/UX Design Systems: End-to-end interface architecture in Figma, including wireframing, component libraries, responsive grids, and interactive prototypes.
  • • Graphic Design & Digital Art: Promotional media, marketing banners, social media assets, and digital art with high aesthetic appeal.
  • • Brand Identity & Strategy: Distinctive visual identities, logo marks, typography pairings, color systems, and comprehensive brand guidelines.

Every engagement follows a structured four-phase roadmap to ensure clarity, velocity, and design precision:

  • • 1. Discovery & Strategy: We analyze your business goals, target audience, technical requirements, and define clear project milestones.
  • • 2. Wireframing & UI/UX Design: I produce interactive prototypes in Figma for layout, visual hierarchy, and user flow feedback before any code is written.
  • • 3. Clean Frontend Engineering: The approved design is built with Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS with regular staging demos.
  • • 4. QA, Optimization & Launch: Cross-browser testing, mobile responsiveness audits, SEO metadata setup, and deployment on Vercel with global CDN.

I work with a modern, production-tested technology stack designed for performance, scalability, and design fidelity:

  • • Frontend Core: Next.js, React, JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and HTML5 semantic markup.
  • • Motion & Canvas: Lenis Smooth Scroll, Three.js WebGL shaders, and CSS GPU-accelerated micro-interactions.
  • • Design & Creative Suite: Figma for comprehensive design systems and wireframes; Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop for visual assets.
  • • Hosting & Infrastructure: Vercel cloud hosting with global edge network, custom DNS routing, SSL certificates, and GitHub CI/CD workflows.

Project timelines vary based on scope, feature set, and content readiness:

  • • Landing Pages & Portfolios: Typically 1 to 2 weeks from kickoff to final live deployment.
  • • Business Platforms & Catalogs: 2 to 4 weeks for multi-page architectures, product category databases, and custom interactions.
  • • Transparent Delivery: Weekly scheduled milestone check-ins, live staging links, and direct communication ensure we launch on schedule.

Getting started is simple, transparent, and hassle-free:

  • • 1. Send an Inquiry: Fill out the contact form below or reach out directly at kgill3662@gmail.com.
  • • 2. Share Project Details: Provide a brief summary of your project, target launch date, required features, and any reference designs.
  • • 3. Proposal & Timeline: I will review your requirements and provide a tailored proposal, timeline estimate, and next steps within 24 hours.
